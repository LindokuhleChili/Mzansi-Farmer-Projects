- **Not South African.** I found no free Pexels photo that is clearly a South African maize field. The two maize photos have no location on their Pexels pages, so do not present them as South African. The storm photo is Kenya, the farmer in the sidebar is Nigeria. Swap in South African photos later if you find or take some.
- **The rejected-screen photo shows a stormy sky over a green wheat field**, not an actual downpour or a farmer in rain. I found no free Pexels or Unsplash farmer-in-rain photo from Africa that fitted.
- **The avatar is a stock portrait** standing in for a made-up user; it must be replaced with a real, consented photo in a real product.
- All names, plots, devices and readings on the screens are fictional example data and are labelled "Example data".
